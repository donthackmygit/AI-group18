THONG BAO NOI BO VE HUONG DAN THU NGHIEM THUE TNCN

Dieu 1. Pham vi ap dung
Tai lieu mau nay dung de kiem thu chuc nang quan ly tai lieu cua he thong chatbot Thue thu nhap ca nhan.

Dieu 2. Noi dung can tra cuu
Thu nhap tu tien luong, tien cong cua ca nhan cu tru la thu nhap chiu thue TNCN theo quy dinh phap luat hien hanh.
Khi tinh thue tu tien luong, tien cong, nguoi nop thue duoc tinh cac khoan giam tru phu hop neu dap ung dieu kien.

Dieu 3. Hieu luc thu nghiem
Tai lieu nay chi dung cho kiem thu pipeline upload, trich xuat, tao chunk va embedding. Khong su dung lam can cu phap ly chinh thuc.
